# Threats to Validity

## Construct validity

Selected metrics may not fully represent test quality, developer productivity, or maintainability. Mitigation includes explicit operational definitions, multiple complementary measures, and independent evaluation criteria.

## Internal validity

Learning effects, condition order, unequal task exposure, prompt differences, manual interventions, and environment drift may affect outcomes. Mitigation may include counterbalancing or randomization, frozen inputs, consistent timing rules, and complete logs.

## External validity

Results from a selected REST API, task set, participant group, or tool configuration may not generalize to other systems or development contexts. The study must describe its context and avoid claims beyond the sampled setting.

## Conclusion validity

Small samples, noisy measurements, multiple comparisons, missing observations, and inappropriate statistical assumptions may produce unreliable conclusions. The analysis plan should specify power considerations, effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, assumptions, and missing-data treatment.

## Reproducibility threats

Hosted AI tools can change, nondeterministic outputs may vary, and exact historical environments may be unavailable. Record dates, identifiers, prompts, parameters, interaction histories, dependency versions, and immutable repository commits wherever permitted.
